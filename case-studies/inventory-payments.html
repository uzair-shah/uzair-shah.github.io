Case study

Inventory payments in Stockflo

Turning supplier payments from spreadsheets and email threads into a tracked approval flow.

Retailo · Product Manager · 2023 · Saudi Arabia, later extended to Pakistan

The problem

  • Supplier payments were tracked in spreadsheets and the accounting tool, with no checks on the data, so the records were hard to trust.
  • 10–15 purchase orders were raised every day. Each payment needed a form filled in by the Commercial team and an approval by email from a Commercial Manager, which was slow and hard to track.
  • Teams spent time chasing each other for proof that payments had gone out, and Finance struggled to produce supplier statements and reconcile accounts.

The goal

  • Record every payment against its purchase order inside Stockflo, our warehouse and inventory system, so supplier statements and reconciliation become easy.
  • Make the payment flow lighter for the people doing it every day.

My role

  • I wrote the product requirements document, defined the user stories and acceptance criteria, and worked with engineering to build the feature in Stockflo.
  • It launched for Saudi Arabia first. A later update extended it to Pakistan.

How a payment moves

RequestCommercial buyer
→
ApproveCommercial manager
→
InitiatedReady to pay
→
PaidPayment executive

A manager can reject a request, and a payment that was recorded wrongly or failed at the bank is marked Void with a reason.

Key decisions

Pay only on locked purchase orders

The request button stays disabled until the purchase order is locked, so nobody pays against an order that can still change.

Show the balance before approving

When a manager approves, they first see total purchases from that supplier, how much has been paid and the balance left, so approvals are made with the full picture.

Proof of payment, or a reason

Marking a payment as paid needs the bank reference and a proof attachment. If there is no attachment, the user picks a reason from a list.

A record of who did what

The system stores who created, approved, paid or voided every payment, giving Finance a full audit trail.

Tax handled in the flow

Withholding tax is set per supplier and calculated automatically on each request. Finance can exclude it, but only by giving a reason that stays visible on the payments screen.

Credit and advance terms

Requests record whether a payment is on credit or in advance; the due date is worked out from the credit days, and the payments list flags what is due.

What I left out, on purpose

Reversing a payment after it was marked paid stayed out of the first release. Payments that went wrong are marked Void with a reason instead, which kept the first version simple.

Outcome

Supplier payments moved out of spreadsheets and email threads into one tracked flow with clear owners at each step. Finance could see what had been paid and what was due, and the procedure was documented for every team involved.
